# Nexora AI - Project Vision & Core Principles

## Tagline
**Enterprise Intelligence. Autonomous Decisions.**

## Executive Summary
Nexora AI is an enterprise-grade AI Operating System designed for modern businesses to convert departmental data into real-time executive intelligence, automated reasoning, continuous monitoring, and data-backed strategic decisions.

Unlike generic chatbot wrappers over document stores, Nexora AI operates as a coordinated multi-agent autonomous executive team. It ingests complex multi-departmental datasets, builds structured Enterprise Memory across relational database tables, vector indices, and key-value caches, and enforces continuous anomaly detection through its proprietary background service, **Sentinel AI**.

---

## Core Mission & Objectives
1. **Zero Hallucination Business Reasoning**: Every insight and recommendation generated by Nexora AI must be verifiable against exact raw data points, SQL queries, or semantic search evidence.
2. **Autonomous Multi-Agent Collaboration**: Five specialized department agents (Sales, Finance, HR, Marketing, Operations) operate independently under the direction of a central Planner Agent. Agents communicate using strict JSON contracts; direct inter-agent chatter is strictly disallowed to ensure auditability and security.
3. **Continuous Sentinel AI Monitoring**: Business parameters (revenue, profit margins, inventory turnover, campaign CAC, employee attrition, operational delays) are continuously evaluated in the background for anomalies, triggering tiered executive alerts.
4. **Data Sovereignty & Local AI Capability**: Built to run entirely open-source (FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis, ChromaDB, SentenceTransformers, Ollama / HuggingFace local models) with zero external paid API dependencies.

---

## Target Personas
- **Chief Executive Officers (CEOs)**: Require high-level cross-departmental correlation, macro trends, and strategic reallocation recommendations.
- **Chief Financial Officers (CFOs)**: Require margin analysis, expense variance tracking, forecasting, and revenue driver attribution.
- **Department VPs / Directors**: Require domain-specific deep dives, operational anomaly alerts, and KPI drill-downs.
- **Enterprise Operations & DevOps**: Require tenant-isolated SaaS architecture, scalable multi-database integration, and containerized deployments.

---

## Non-Negotiable Product Standards
- **No Natural Language Inter-Agent Messages**: All internal agent communications use strictly validated Pydantic JSON schemas.
- **Strict Out-of-Domain Boundaries**: Nexora AI explicitly rejects non-corporate topics (politics, general software debugging, medical, personal advice).
- **Luxury Enterprise UI Standard**: Minimalist dark palette (`#08080A`), glassmorphism, Framer Motion animations, custom Recharts, bento grid layout, and keyboard-driven command palettes (`Ctrl+K`).
